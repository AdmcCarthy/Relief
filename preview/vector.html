Relief · proof of concept · Vestland

Topography and bathymetry, generalised for data maps

Copernicus DEM (land) and the EMODnet Bathymetry DTM (sea floor), merged, projected to UTM 32N and contoured into stacked layers at three levels of detail. Coastlines are generalised by scale: islands and lakes too small to see are dropped and skerry fields merge.
